# Make a song

A song on the Device is a **tape**: a stack of **tracks** that play together, each one a take you kept. This guide builds the **Starter song** the Device opens with, step by step. First come the Suspended chords in D, strummed on the oscillator three times round. Then a New jack swing beat comes in on the second bar and plays to the end. Last, the same strum plays once more over the middle round, on an oscillator with a saw wave under its sine. After that, the guide shows what else the tracks can do.

## Start a new tape

Your tapes live in **Tapes** (`T`), a tile each, every one with its own tracks, tempo and time signature.

![Tapes: the Starter song, 3 tracks at 96 BPM in 4/4](../../public/docs/user-manual/tapes.webp)

Press **Save** for a new tape at the tempo playing now. (The green knob or ← → opens another tape, Play plays it, and Delete removes the open one, after asking.)

![Save started Tape 1, beside the Starter song](../../public/docs/user-manual/new-tape.webp)

Press **Tracks** (`Esc`) to see it: a new tape holds just the **Take**, the row for whatever you're working on.

![Tape 1's tracks: just the take](../../public/docs/user-manual/new-tape-tracks.webp)

## The chords: Suspended on the oscillator

A **chord progression** is a short run of chords that a song repeats. The Device knows dozens (see [Chord progressions](../library/chord-progressions.md)). It loads one as the take in whatever key you like, a chord a bar, in your **play style**: how a chord's notes come out.

**1. Pick a play style.** Press **Play style** (`Q`), turn the red knob to the **Strum** page and pick **Folk strum**: down, down-up, up-down-up over a bar of eighths, as a guitarist's hand goes, with the first down leaned on.

![Play style's Strum page, Folk strum picked at 1/8](../../public/docs/user-manual/folk-strum.webp)

**2. Open the progressions.** Hold **Shift** and press the **Piano roll** pad (`Shift V`), then turn to the **Cadences** page. **Suspended** (Isus4 I Isus2 I) never leaves the home chord. It lifts the chord's third to the fourth and lets it fall back, then drops it to the second and lets it rise back. Tap it or press Play to hear it in its own key, or play a key to hear it from that note. Previews play a little faster than the tape's tempo.

![The progressions' Cadences page, Suspended picked](../../public/docs/user-manual/progressions.webp)

**3. Save it.** Press **Save**. The screen asks for the **home note**: the key the song will be in.

![After Save: Play the home note](../../public/docs/user-manual/progression-home-note.webp)

**4. Play D.** Press the D above middle C (`H` on the computer keys). Suspended becomes the **take** in D: Dsus4, D, Dsus2 and D, a bar each, in the folk strum. The **piano roll** opens on it: a row per note, time running left to right.

![Suspended in D as the take, each chord strummed down, down-up, up-down-up](../../public/docs/user-manual/progression-on-the-take.webp)

**5. Pick the oscillator.** The take plays on whatever instrument is on the keys, so you can change it now. Hold **Shift** and press instrument pad 1 (`Shift 1`) for the **Oscillator**, the Grand piano's alternate. Picking an alternate brings it to the top of its pad: from now on pad 1 plays the oscillator, and Shift + pad 1 the piano.

![Shift + pad 1 put the take on the Oscillator](../../public/docs/user-manual/oscillator.webp)

Press **Play** to hear it loop, and Stop (`` ` ``) when you're done.

![The take playing](../../public/docs/user-manual/take-playing.webp)

**6. Keep it.** Press **Save** in the piano roll. The take becomes track #1, on the oscillator, and the Tracks view opens with it picked.

![Track #1 Oscillator](../../public/docs/user-manual/progression-saved-as-track.webp)

**7. Play it three times.** **Shift + →** plays the picked track again right after itself. Press it twice and the four bars go round three times, marked ×2 and ×3: twelve bars. (Shift + ← plays it fewer times.)

![Shift + → twice: the chords play three times, marked ×2 and ×3](../../public/docs/user-manual/repeat.webp)

## The drums: New jack swing

**1. Pick a beat.** Hold **Shift** and press the **Drum grid** pad (`Shift B`). The red knob turns the styles (simple, rock, pop, funk and soul, jazz, Latin and reggae, hip hop, electronic, classical and South Asian) and the blue knob picks a beat. Picking one also puts its kit on the keys, and a tap or Play previews it. On the **Funk and soul** page, pick **New jack swing**: swung hats, a kick that skips around the beat, and a snare doubled by a clap on two and four, on the 808 kit. See [Drum beats](../library/drum-beats.md) for them all.

![The beats' Funk and soul page, New jack swing picked](../../public/docs/user-manual/beats.webp)

**2. Save it.** Press **Save**: the beat loads into the **drum grid** on its kit. The grid has a row per drum and a column per step. Here the steps are sixteenth-note triplets (1/16T), six to a beat, which is what makes it swing.

![New jack swing in the drum grid on the 808 kit](../../public/docs/user-manual/drum-grid.webp)

Press **Play** to loop it. Stopped, the green knob moves the head and the keys set or clear a drum there; the red knob sets the length and the blue one the resolution. **Record** loops it too and lands what you tap on the nearest step, so you can play a part in as it goes round; Play alone only plays. Pick another kit and the pattern comes along: on the madal or the tabla, the kick plays as the open bass stroke, the snare as a slap on it and the hats on the treble, and back again.

![The drum grid playing](../../public/docs/user-manual/drum-grid-playing.webp)

**3. Keep it.** Press **Save** again: track #2, picked.

![Track #2 808 kit under the chords](../../public/docs/user-manual/beat-saved-as-track.webp)

**4. Bring it in on bar 2, and play it to the end.** **→** slides the picked track a bar later, so the beat starts on bar 2, after a bar of chords on their own. The beat is one bar long, and from bar 2 to the end of the chords is eleven bars. Press **Shift + →** ten times, until the row reads ×11. The footer now says Starts 2 · ×11.

![→ once, then Shift + → ten times: the beat from bar 2 to the end of the chords](../../public/docs/user-manual/drums-placed.webp)

## The second oscillator: a saw under the sine

**1. Load Suspended again.** Press **Tracks** for the main screen. `Shift V` opens the progressions on Suspended: press **Save**, then **D**, and the same strum is the take on the piano roll. The beat left the 808 kit on the keys, so press pad 1 (`1`) for the oscillator again.

**2. Add a second wave.** Press **Synth** (`Z`) for the oscillator's synth parameters, and **→** to open the **Exciter** page: its two waves, how loud the second is and where it sits against the first. Turn the green knob to **Wave 2: Saw** and the blue one to **Phase: 216°**. The sine and the saw now play together, the saw's buzz under the sine's round tone. Press Synth again to close it, then **Piano roll** (`V`) for the take.

![The Exciter page: Wave 1 Sine, Wave 2 Saw, Osc 2 level 50%, Phase 216°](../../public/docs/user-manual/sine-and-saw.webp)

**3. Keep it once, over the middle round.** Press **Save** for track #3, then **→** four times: it starts on bar 5 and plays once, over the first oscillator's second round. Press **Play** to hear the whole tape: this is the Starter song.

![Track #3 Oscillator on bar 5, over the middle round](../../public/docs/user-manual/second-oscillator.webp)

![The Starter song playing](../../public/docs/user-manual/starter-song.webp)

## Manage the tracks

**Pick a track** with ↑ ↓ or the blue knob. The footer says where the picked one starts, how many times it plays, its tempo and its volume.

![↓ picks the 808 kit: it starts on bar 2 and plays eleven times](../../public/docs/user-manual/pick-a-track.webp)

**Copies** play a whole track again further along the same lane, so a part can rest and come back. **Shift + ↓** adds a copy after the last, picked, and **→** slides the picked copy along a bar a press. Here two presses leave a two-bar gap where track #3 rests, and then it comes back for the song's last two bars and two more after them. Shift + ↑ and ↓ step between a track's copies, and the ×2 on the row's right edge counts them. A copy never slides back over the one before it, and one that grows pushes the later ones along.

![Shift + ↓ then → →: a copy of track #3 two bars after the first](../../public/docs/user-manual/add-a-copy.webp)

**Click a copy** to pick it. ← → then slide just that copy, Shift + ← → play it more or fewer times, and Delete takes it out.

![Clicking track #3's first copy picks it](../../public/docs/user-manual/click-a-copy.webp)

**Mute** (`,`) silences the picked track: its `M` lights and its lane dims. Press it again to bring it back.

![The 808 kit muted: its M lit and its lane dim](../../public/docs/user-manual/mute.webp)

**Shift + Mute** solos it instead, so you hear only that track. Shift + Mute again lets the rest back in.

![The 808 kit soloed: its S lit, every other lane dim](../../public/docs/user-manual/solo.webp)

**A track's tempo.** Press **Tempo** (`M`) with a track picked to see its own tempo: a track keeps the tempo it was saved at, so changing the tape's tempo later leaves it be. Press **↑** to link it to the tape's tempo instead, so it speeds up and slows down with the tape.

![Tempo with the 808 kit picked: its own tempo, 96 BPM](../../public/docs/user-manual/track-tempo.webp)

**A track's instrument.** An instrument pad with a track picked puts that sound on it: here pad 6 turned the 808 kit into the Madal, and pad 5 turns it back. The notes stay as they were played, and a drum track's strokes carry over between the kits, the madal and the tabla.

![Pad 6 with the 808 kit picked: the track becomes the Madal, its beat carried over](../../public/docs/user-manual/track-instrument.webp)

**Clip** (`X`) loops a section of a track instead of playing it whole; the section is outlined in red. Hold Shift and the knobs change job: red and blue move the clip's start and end, and green slides the track by beats. Shift + Clip cuts the track down to its clip for good, and the track leaves clip mode: it now plays whole, which is the clip.

![Clip on the drums: the beat's bar outlined in red](../../public/docs/user-manual/clip.webp)

![Clip with Shift held: the footer shows Clip start, Slide and Clip end](../../public/docs/user-manual/clip-shift.webp)

**Delete** asks first: press it once and the screen asks you to press again. With a copy picked it deletes just that copy; a track's only copy deletes the track.

![Delete asks: Press again to delete 808 kit](../../public/docs/user-manual/delete.webp)

Everything the Tracks view does:

| To                     | Press                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |

| Set its volume         | the red knob                                                 |
| Slide it by a bar      | ← or →                                                       |
| Play it more times     | Shift + → (and Shift + ← for fewer)                          |
| Pick its next copy     | Shift + ↓, which past the last adds one; Shift + ↑ goes back |
| Pick a copy directly   | click it in the lane                                         |
| Change its instrument  | an instrument pad                                            |
| Set its tempo          | Tempo (`M`), with the track picked                           |
| Set its effects        | ADSR, LFO or FX, with the track picked                       |

## Record your own playing

Pick a sound (here the Grand piano: Shift + pad 1 brings it back to the top of its pad) and open the **Piano roll** (`V`). Press **Record** (`.`) to arm it, then **Play** to start.

![Armed: Press Play to record](../../public/docs/user-manual/record-armed.webp)

What you play becomes the take, just as you played it, in time with the click if the metronome is on.

![Recording: Rec in the status line, the notes appearing as they're played](../../public/docs/user-manual/recording.webp)

Press Record again to stop. The take loops in whole bars; stopped, the green knob scrolls through it, the blue knob moves the roll's octave and ↑ ↓ move it a semitone.

![The take on the roll](../../public/docs/user-manual/take.webp)

Press **Save** to keep it as the next track, and carry on: more progressions, beats and takes, copies and gaps, until the song is done. Then press Save in Tapes for the next one.

![The take saved as track #4 Grand piano](../../public/docs/user-manual/take-saved.webp)
